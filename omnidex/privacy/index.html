Omnidex — Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Who we are

Omnidex is an unofficial, fan-made creature encyclopedia. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Game Freak or Creatures Inc. Omnidex has no accounts and no servers of its own.

What stays on your device

Favorites, teams, your dex, shiny counters and preferences are stored only on your device. We never receive them. A backup file is created only when you export one, and goes wherever you choose to send it.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address and basic device information to serve, measure and personalise ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions that require it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change it any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. On iOS, personalised ads also depend on your App Tracking Transparency choice. See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

Omnidex PRO is a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, handled by the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether PRO is active; it receives an anonymous app user id and your purchase receipt, never your name or payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Online content

Large artwork, 3D models and trading-card images are downloaded from public sources (GitHub, pokemontcg.io) when you open them. These services see your IP address, like any website you visit.

Children

Omnidex is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. Ads are requested with the settings Google provides for a general audience.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete. You can reset your ad identifier in your device settings and withdraw ad consent in the app at any time.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: contatodbzera@gmail.com.
